# Teal Tidal

A small browser extension that tracks TIDAL web playback and publishes qualifying
listens to your Bluesky-hosted account for teal.fm. Published listens are public.
No Last.fm account or separate scrobbling server is needed.

- [Download and installation](https://github.com/IHIutch/teal-tidal/releases)
- [Privacy policy](privacy.html)
- [Support](https://github.com/IHIutch/teal-tidal/issues)

Independent beta software. Not affiliated with TIDAL, Bluesky, or teal.fm.
