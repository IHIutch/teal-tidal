# Teal listening eligibility

Researched 2026-11-20 against official source, Git refs, release metadata, and package manifests. Local baseline is `7c2ce07f65d1697814ea5df854dd27a4038d0607`. No implementation changes.

## Finding

Teal's lexicon and Piper's implemented Spotify rule disagree. The local formula is a defensible interpretation of the lexicon, but it does **not** match released Piper. Neither source establishes a universal, executable Teal rule with a 120-second floor.

The [Teal lexicon at GitHub commit `455634384151580947f31b743b689fb8d6d2427e`](https://github.com/teal-fm/teal/blob/455634384151580947f31b743b689fb8d6d2427e/lexicons/fm.teal/feed/play.json) describes full-track listening below two minutes and half-duration listening with a four-minute cap. Its final "whichever is longest" clause is ambiguous. The same description exists in the directly cloned [Tangled snapshot `a033f8ee9625401d7c50663e44bb085899781d32`](https://tangled.org/teal.fm/teal/tree/a033f8ee9625401d7c50663e44bb085899781d32). The schema contains no listened-time field or validation that enforces this prose; duration itself is optional.

Local `threshold(d) = max(min(d,120), min(d/2,240))`, with `listened >= threshold`, means:

- Below 120 seconds, listen for the full duration.
- From 120 through 240 seconds, listen for 120 seconds.
- From 240 through 480 seconds, listen for half the duration.
- Above 480 seconds, listen for 240 seconds.

The 120-second plateau follows from this formula. It is an interpretation of the lexicon's longest-of wording, not a numeric rule independently confirmed by official tracking code. Reading the two-minute condition as an exclusive branch would instead give `d < 120 ? d : min(d/2,240)`. The prose alone does not settle that difference.

## Actual Piper source and releases

Both [Tangled Piper `f2af52b774dc1fb3476ae5b618624e50f90b975e`](https://tangled.org/teal.fm/piper/tree/f2af52b774dc1fb3476ae5b618624e50f90b975e) and [released Piper v0.2.1 Spotify code](https://github.com/teal-fm/piper/blob/edf0963e5db13115d836c21144f02c0dc051ff5b/service/spotify/spotify.go#L709-L729) compute `max(durationMs/2, 30000)` and require accumulated milliseconds to be **strictly greater**. There is no 120-second floor or 240-second cap. Integer division rounds half-duration down to a millisecond. The [threshold tests](https://github.com/teal-fm/piper/blob/edf0963e5db13115d836c21144f02c0dc051ff5b/service/spotify/spotify_test.go#L512-L583) explicitly cover the 30-second floor and rejection at the exact threshold.

## Libraries and dependency versions

Piper implements eligibility in its own service. Its [v0.2.1 `go.mod`](https://github.com/teal-fm/piper/blob/edf0963e5db13115d836c21144f02c0dc051ff5b/go.mod) has no shared Teal threshold dependency. Its AT Protocol dependency is Indigo `v0.0.0-20251003000214-3259b215110e`; generated Teal types and lexicon JSON live in Piper itself. The npm manifest only declares Tailwind dependencies.

Teal's [`@teal/lexicons` manifest](https://github.com/teal-fm/teal/blob/455634384151580947f31b743b689fb8d6d2427e/packages/lexicons/package.json) is a workspace package without a package version. The [lockfile](https://github.com/teal-fm/teal/blob/455634384151580947f31b743b689fb8d6d2427e/pnpm-lock.yaml) resolves its dependencies to `@atproto/api@0.20.5`, `@atproto/lex-cli@0.5.7`, `@atproto/lexicon@0.7.1`, and `@atproto/xrpc-server@0.7.19`. These do not replace the service's explicit eligibility expression. The [npm registry lookup for `@teal/lexicons`](https://registry.npmjs.org/%40teal%2Flexicons) returned `Not found` on the research date. No published official threshold helper was identified in the inspected manifests. This is not a claim that every Teal-related third-party package was audited.

## Edge cases and concrete mismatches

Compared directly with the pinned Piper Spotify code, local thresholds differ for short tracks, tracks between two and four minutes, tracks over eight minutes, and exact-boundary acceptance. For example, local 90-, 180-, and 600-second tracks require 90, 120, and 240 seconds; Piper requires more than 45, 90, and 300 seconds respectively. A 240-second track needs at least 120 seconds locally and strictly more than 120 seconds in Piper. These are Piper compatibility mismatches, not proof that the local implementation violates a settled Teal specification.

- **Unknown duration:** Local `validSample` rejects nonfinite, zero, or negative duration before tracking. Calling `threshold(0)` alone returns zero, but that is not the accepted-sample path. The lexicon permits omitted duration without prescribing a fallback. Piper's Spotify expression gives a 30-second threshold at zero duration, but its repeat logic also resets `hasStamped` each poll because accumulated time is always at least zero. Do not copy this as a deliberate unknown-duration policy. Its [zero-duration test](https://github.com/teal-fm/piper/blob/edf0963e5db13115d836c21144f02c0dc051ff5b/service/spotify/spotify_test.go#L592-L612) only checks no initial stamp.
- **Short-track completion:** Local `advance` starts credited listening at zero on the first sample and drops the old state on a track change or detected loop. Missing the beginning or final sample can prevent a genuinely complete short play from qualifying. There is no completion-event allowance. Piper Spotify has no completion exception either; it subtracts duration and clears its stamp flag before checking eligibility, which also makes very short tracks problematic within a normal single play cycle.
- **Tolerance:** Local seek checks allow progress up to elapsed time plus 1.25 seconds and credit up to elapsed plus 0.25 seconds. Those are accumulation tolerances, not reductions to the eligibility threshold. Piper instead seeds up to 30 seconds from initial position and adds wall-clock poll deltas capped at 30 seconds, preserving paused state. Neither inspected source establishes an official end-of-track grace period. See [Piper state updates](https://github.com/teal-fm/piper/blob/edf0963e5db13115d836c21144f02c0dc051ff5b/service/spotify/spotify.go#L630-L729) and local [tracker.ts](/Users/jbhutch/Sites/teal-tidal/src/tracker.ts:8).
- **Provider differences:** Piper is not internally universal. Its [Apple Music conversion](https://github.com/teal-fm/piper/blob/edf0963e5db13115d836c21144f02c0dc051ff5b/service/applemusic/applemusic.go#L385-L415) assumes recent-history entries are full plays and stamps positive durations above 30 seconds. Its [Last.fm importer](https://github.com/teal-fm/piper/blob/edf0963e5db13115d836c21144f02c0dc051ff5b/service/lastfm/lastfm.go#L460-L480) marks already-scrobbled history as stamped rather than measuring listening again.

## Minimal recommendation

For the stated goal of following Teal's lexicon, retain the numeric formula pending an authoritative clarification. Change the comment's unconditional claim of matching Teal into an explicit statement that this implements the longest-of interpretation, including the 120-second plateau. Do not substitute Web Scrobbler rules or silently adopt Piper's contradictory provider-specific rule.

The concrete behavioral issue worth fixing separately is missed short-track completion. A verified end-of-track event or carefully bounded accounting could address it, but the inspected sources do not justify a particular grace period. If exact Piper Spotify compatibility becomes the explicit requirement, the minimal numeric change would instead be `max(d/2,30)` with strict `>` comparison, preserving the distinction from lexicon compliance.

## Multi-Scrobbler implementation evidence

Checked 2026-11-20 against FoxxMD's upstream master, pinned to [`d164f568a47874e7a8d33604db630e6edbfdcca6`](https://github.com/FoxxMD/multi-scrobbler/commit/d164f568a47874e7a8d33604db630e6edbfdcca6). This is primary evidence for a community client's behavior, not an official Teal specification.

- The monitored-listening defaults are **240 seconds OR 50 percent**, configurable per source. Both comparisons include equality. The exact predicate is `secondsTracked >= 240 || Math.round(secondsTracked / playDuration * 100) >= 50`, when duration is known and nonzero. Consequently, 49.5% already passes the percentage branch; describing this as exactly half would miss the rounding. There is no minimum track-duration gate or mandatory 30-second listening floor in this helper. Missing or zero duration skips percentage and requires at least 240 tracked seconds by default. Explicit `null` options also fall back to defaults because the assignments use `??`. See [constants](https://github.com/FoxxMD/multi-scrobbler/blob/d164f568a47874e7a8d33604db630e6edbfdcca6/src/backend/common/infrastructure/Atomic.ts#L125-L134), [executable predicate](https://github.com/FoxxMD/multi-scrobbler/blob/d164f568a47874e7a8d33604db630e6edbfdcca6/src/backend/utils/TimeUtils.ts#L224-L254), and [configuration documentation](https://github.com/FoxxMD/multi-scrobbler/blob/d164f568a47874e7a8d33604db630e6edbfdcca6/docsite/src/components/snippets/_scrobble-threshold.mdx).
- Eligibility belongs to the source. `MemorySource` checks the previous play on a track change, or the current play when stale, then applies that helper and duplicate checks. This is the monitored-listening path, not a claim that imported scrobble history is remeasured. See [candidate selection](https://github.com/FoxxMD/multi-scrobbler/blob/d164f568a47874e7a8d33604db630e6edbfdcca6/src/backend/sources/MemorySource.ts#L308-L324) and [eligibility call](https://github.com/FoxxMD/multi-scrobbler/blob/d164f568a47874e7a8d33604db630e6edbfdcca6/src/backend/sources/MemorySource.ts#L357-L396).
- Positional tracking has a **strictly less than three seconds** end allowance. When finalizing a nonempty, noninitial range while calculated status is playing or stale, known nonzero duration with `duration - lastPosition < 3` sets the range's final position to track duration. Exactly three seconds remaining fails this condition. This adjusts the range endpoint, not total credited listening to a full track. The test also lacks a nonnegative lower bound, so an overshooting position passes. A new range starting while already playing at position `<= 3` starts at position **1**, not zero. Thus even an inferred finish can retain an initial one-second deficit. See [start adjustment and finalization](https://github.com/FoxxMD/multi-scrobbler/blob/d164f568a47874e7a8d33604db630e6edbfdcca6/src/backend/sources/PlayerState/PositionalPlayerState.ts#L49-L124). Polling also targets two seconds before the expected end, with a one-second minimum interval, in [MemorySource](https://github.com/FoxxMD/multi-scrobbler/blob/d164f568a47874e7a8d33604db630e6edbfdcca6/src/backend/sources/MemorySource.ts#L401-L434).
- Paused/stopped reports close the current range; resumption starts another. Positional ranges count endpoint differences, and the player sums ranges. Backward movement or absolute drift beyond the allowed real-time drift starts a new range rather than crediting the seek jump. The positional player defaults to 3,000 ms allowed drift, with equality tolerated. Unchanged position eventually produces calculated paused status. Generic tracking instead measures timestamp differences. These are sampling heuristics, not exact playback-event accounting; range finalization can also occur on pause or seek, so the end allowance does not prove natural completion. See [state transitions](https://github.com/FoxxMD/multi-scrobbler/blob/d164f568a47874e7a8d33604db630e6edbfdcca6/src/backend/sources/PlayerState/AbstractPlayerState.ts#L222-L269), [range sum](https://github.com/FoxxMD/multi-scrobbler/blob/d164f568a47874e7a8d33604db630e6edbfdcca6/src/backend/sources/PlayerState/AbstractPlayerState.ts#L355-L364), [seek detection](https://github.com/FoxxMD/multi-scrobbler/blob/d164f568a47874e7a8d33604db630e6edbfdcca6/src/backend/sources/PlayerState/ListenRange.ts#L125-L159), [drift default and pause detection](https://github.com/FoxxMD/multi-scrobbler/blob/d164f568a47874e7a8d33604db630e6edbfdcca6/src/backend/sources/PlayerState/PositionalPlayerState.ts#L20-L83), and [duration calculation](https://github.com/FoxxMD/multi-scrobbler/blob/d164f568a47874e7a8d33604db630e6edbfdcca6/src/backend/sources/PlayerState/ListenProgress.ts#L19-L51).
- Neither `TealfmScrobbler.ts` nor `TealApiClient.ts` adds a Teal listening threshold. The scrobbler converts the play and calls `createScrobbleRecord`; the API client posts `fm.teal.feed.play` to the PDS. Its conversion rounds track duration but does not check listened time. The adapter's 20-second `nowPlayingMinThreshold` is status-update spacing, not eligibility. See [adapter constructor](https://github.com/FoxxMD/multi-scrobbler/blob/d164f568a47874e7a8d33604db630e6edbfdcca6/src/backend/scrobblers/TealfmScrobbler.ts#L48-L56), [submission](https://github.com/FoxxMD/multi-scrobbler/blob/d164f568a47874e7a8d33604db630e6edbfdcca6/src/backend/scrobblers/TealfmScrobbler.ts#L130-L149), [PDS call](https://github.com/FoxxMD/multi-scrobbler/blob/d164f568a47874e7a8d33604db630e6edbfdcca6/src/backend/common/vendor/teal/TealApiClient.ts#L111-L131), and [record conversion](https://github.com/FoxxMD/multi-scrobbler/blob/d164f568a47874e7a8d33604db630e6edbfdcca6/src/backend/common/vendor/teal/TealApiClient.ts#L240-L264).

The [latest stable release, 0.21.1](https://github.com/FoxxMD/multi-scrobbler/releases/tag/0.21.1), was published November 17, 2026, at commit `0fa664c2ec752eb1de7084e7560cba9ea9e7cd19`. Direct inspection confirms the same [rounded OR predicate](https://github.com/FoxxMD/multi-scrobbler/blob/0fa664c2ec752eb1de7084e7560cba9ea9e7cd19/src/backend/utils/TimeUtils.ts#L238-L268), [three-second finalization](https://github.com/FoxxMD/multi-scrobbler/blob/0fa664c2ec752eb1de7084e7560cba9ea9e7cd19/src/backend/sources/PlayerState/PositionalPlayerState.ts#L87-L124), and [Teal submission without a threshold override](https://github.com/FoxxMD/multi-scrobbler/blob/0fa664c2ec752eb1de7084e7560cba9ea9e7cd19/src/backend/scrobblers/TealfmScrobbler.ts#L129-L149). This does not assert the entire release and master are identical.

This does **not resolve the lexicon/Piper disagreement**. Multi-Scrobbler supplies a third policy, derived from Last.fm guidance and configurable by the user. It neither requires full listening below two minutes nor imposes Piper's 30-second floor. For our short-track issue, it supplies precedent for bounded final-position inference and faster end polling, not authority to change Teal's threshold. Evaluate the old track before discarding it on transition, and handle missed beginning and ending samples separately. Copying its three-second endpoint bump alone will not satisfy our full-duration rule when initial listening was missed. Any chosen completion allowance should be documented as our accounting policy, with pause, seek, exact-boundary, and initial-gap cases checked explicitly.

## Local implementation follow-up

The threshold formula is unchanged. Initial playing samples at positions zero
through one second now include that small initial interval. Before a track change
or repeat resets the state, the worker evaluates the outgoing play. It can credit
a tail below three seconds only if the previous play was active, all earlier
position progress was credited, the next play is active within its first three
seconds, and elapsed time covers the tail plus the next position within a maximum
six-second sampling gap. This is a local inference policy, not an official Teal
allowance or proof of natural completion. Larger missing starts remain uncredited.
Tracker and worker tests cover completion, repeats, duplicate prevention, pauses,
seeks, late starts, timing boundaries, and long gaps.
